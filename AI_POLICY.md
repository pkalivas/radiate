# AI Usage Policy

AI tools are increasingly being used in the industry & while they can be useful, they are not a replacement for human judgment. Contributions made with them still need a person who understands and stands behind the work. These rules apply to pull requests, issues, and comments.

- **Disclose any AI use.** Name the tool(s) you used (e.g. Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot)
  and say what they did: wrote the code, wrote the tests, drafted the description, etc.
  Autocomplete of a line or two doesn't need a mention.

- **A human is responsible for every submission.** Accounts run by autonomous agents
  may not open issues or pull requests, or comment. If an agent helped you, you
  submit the work and answer the review - we're not going to have a conversation with your agent.

- **You must have reviewed and run what you submit.** Read every change and run the
  tests locally (`just test`). Claims in a PR or issue, such as benchmark numbers,
  reproductions, or "this fixes X", must come from runs you did yourself.

- **Keep it short and edit the output.** AI text tends to be long. Trim issues,
  PR descriptions, and code comments to what a reviewer needs. 

- **Discuss larger changes first.** For anything beyond a small fix, open an issue
  and wait for a maintainer to respond before sending an AI-assisted PR.

If AI use looks likely but wasn't disclosed, the PR or issue may be closed without review.

## Why

Radiate is maintained by a small number of people. Reviewing slop PRs or having conversations with other people's AI agents is a waste of time and effort. Disclosure tells reviewers where to look closely.
